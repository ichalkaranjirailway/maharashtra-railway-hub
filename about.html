The Maharashtra Railway Information Hub tracks railway projects, services and official developments across Maharashtra, with a permanently featured tracker for the Hatkanangale–Ichalkaranji railway connectivity project. It is built to be a long-term, evidence-based public archive rather than a news feed or a campaign site.

Every project record carries a status history rather than a single current value, so figures such as cost or sanction status can be traced over time instead of being silently overwritten. Every update and status change is expected to carry a source and a verification level:

  • 🟢 Verified — confirmed against an official source
  • 🟡 Under verification — reported, confirmation in progress
  • 🔵 Historical / archived — no longer current, kept for the record
  • ⚪ Source-based report — attributed to media or a public source, not yet independently confirmed
  • 🔴 Unverified — never published as fact

Nothing in this build is presented to the public as a 🔴 unverified fact.

Primary sources (Ministry of Railways, Railway Board, Central Railway, Government of Maharashtra, official tender/procurement notices) are prioritized over media reporting, which is in turn prioritized over public submissions. Media-derived items are always labelled as media reporting; publicly submitted items go through a quarantine → review → verification → publish workflow before appearing on the live site (see README.md for the intended pipeline).

Demo data notice
